## Acquisition, storage and edit lineage

After approval, acquire each named container once, capture its catalogue/licence notices and
hash untouched original bytes. Inspect archives without executing contents; record exact
member names and hashes before selecting the relevant sound. The JSON has `null` hashes,
trim windows and measurements deliberately: none have been acquired or measured yet.

Known catalogue download sizes total approximately 216.7948 MB;
Kenney's pack size is not stated. Propose a **500 MB source-download cap** and **2 GB working
storage cap**. Three five-minute 48 kHz stereo PCM24 mixes require about 259.2 MB before
headers; originals, editable projects and excerpts consume the remainder. These are planning
limits, not measured sizes or total labour/network costs. Pause on a cap, missing rights,
access failure or an unqualified class; do not buy or generate replacements automatically.

Keep originals, rights notices, editable projects/scripts, excerpts and mixes under the future
external `audio-direction/material-audition/` package or a disposable workspace. Never put
candidate audio in `game/`. Use available local `ffmpeg`/`ffprobe`; a disposable audition may
reuse the approved mixer layout while ordinary Main and runtime assets stay unchanged.
Any audition-specific tooling belongs to the external package. No package installation or
new provider is needed for this proposed method.

For each edit, record source/container/member hashes, source metadata, exact trim and loop
sample positions, crossfades, resampling/channel conversion, ordered gain/EQ/dynamics/fades,
tool versions/parameters, editable-project hash, export hash, credits and measurements.
Keep 48 kHz editable masters at suitable precision and export PCM24 WAV for review. The timber
and leaf-rain sources are MP3: PCM exports are lossless encodings of those edits, not recovery
of a lossless original. Unknown archive formats stay unknown until inspected.

Prepare the entire series before one owner listening review. Local qualification listening
would be part of the requested next scope; it does not create separate owner approval gates
for individual sounds. Reject modern noise, added animals/voices, startling transients,
dense music and inappropriate source claims. If any slot cannot qualify, record the gap
and finish source resolution before presenting a complete series; no unapproved fallback
recording, AI generation or paid acquisition is inferred.
